# Contributors / 贡献者

OCG Manager is maintained by [Klarkxy](https://github.com/klarkxy) and improved
by community contributions. Thank you to everyone who reports issues, proposes
changes, reviews code, and helps validate releases.

OCG Manager 由 [Klarkxy](https://github.com/klarkxy) 维护，也受益于社区的代码
贡献、问题反馈、审查与发布验证。感谢每一位参与者。
